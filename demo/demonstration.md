## How to Test AI Analysis Features

1. **Access the Dashboard**: Go to the ExamCraft main page
2. **Navigate to Results**: Click on the "Results" tab
3. **Find Test Submission**: Look for submission ID 1 with violations
4. **Open AI Analysis**: Click the brain icon (🧠) next to the submission
5. **Explore Features**: 
   - View detected violations with timestamps
   - Generate AI reports using Gemini
   - Analyze video recordings
   - Review timeline of events
